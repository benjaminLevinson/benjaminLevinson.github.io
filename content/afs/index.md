Free Member Monday screenings at Austin Film Society, in your calendar. This independent feed follows the specific dates AFS announces; it is not operated by AFS.

Membership and ticket reservations are handled by AFS. Open an event’s source link before making plans. A sold-out screening stays on the calendar.

The published schedule is checked every six hours. Updates then reach this site through its hourly build, followed by your calendar app’s own refresh schedule. Failed checks preserve the last successful calendar.
