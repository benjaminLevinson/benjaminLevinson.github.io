Subscribe to Central Austin’s yoga, fitness, climbing lessons, and public events. This independent calendar uses published Crux bookings; it is not operated by Crux.

The calendar covers the source’s rolling one-month window. It includes sold-out sessions, and excludes birthday parties, camps, youth programs, Parents Night Out, and pod reservations. Follow each event’s source link for availability and booking requirements.

Refreshes are attempted hourly. Your calendar app chooses when to fetch updates. If collection fails or needs a security check, the last successful calendar stays available; check the freshness notice below before relying on it.
